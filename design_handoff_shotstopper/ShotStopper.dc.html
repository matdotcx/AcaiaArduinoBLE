ShotStopper · Visual System v1
A calm instrument for brew‑by‑weight espresso.
One cohesive system applied across the phone and the watch kiosk. Glanceable, wet‑handed, read from across the counter. The bones are untouched — Live / History / Settings stay where they are. This pass elevates the visual layer: a numeric hierarchy with live weight as the hero, a parseable extraction curve, intentional light & dark, and a state language of grey idle → orange brewing → green done.

07 — Recipes
one identity across settings · history · detail
A recipe bundles the per-coffee settings we control over BLE — target weight, auto-tare, min/max duration, drip delay. Every shot pulled while it's active is tagged with it, so you can filter History to compare months of one coffee. The single highest-leverage move: give each recipe a color + icon that travels everywhere. (Terminology unified to “Recipe” — the old “Presets” label is retired.)
